## Tensor singular value-preserving norm for robust visual data recovery

### Datasets
The experiments in our paper are evaluated on the following public datasets. You can download them here:
- **RGB Images (BSD):** [Berkeley Segmentation Dataset](https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Research/Projects/CS/vision/bsds/)
- **Hyperspectral Images (HSIs):** [Remote Sensing Scenes](https://www.ehu.eus/ccwintco/index.php/Hyperspectral_Remote_Sensing_Scenes)
- **Multispectral Images (MSIs):** [CAVE Dataset](https://cave.cs.columbia.edu/repository)
- **Color Videos:** [YUV Video Sequences](https://trace.eas.asu.edu/yuv/index.html)

### Requirements
- MATLAB R2023a or later
- [Tensor Toolbox](https://www.tensortoolbox.org/) (Required for baseline comparisons and some auxiliary tensor operations)
